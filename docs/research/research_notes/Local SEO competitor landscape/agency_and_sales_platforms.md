# Agency operating platforms and agency sales/prospecting tools (competitors to OpenMarko's agency + prospecting side)

_Researched 2026-10-30. Prices were read from vendor pages on that date unless a source date is given. Third-party aggregators are labeled. Lines under "Inferences" are reasoning, not sourced fact. This note does not repeat the prior note (`docs/research/origami-and-openmarko-gtm.md`), which covers Origami, Instantly, Smartlead, Apify Google Maps pricing ($1.50/1k places plus add-ons), Hunter, ZeroBounce, Twilio Lookup and DataForSEO Business Listings._

## Q1. Fact sheets: what each platform offers for local SEO and for selling local clients, how it is built, pricing, ICP, company, reviews

### Takeaway
GoHighLevel and Vendasta are the two "agency operating systems". Both now bundle a prospecting audit, CRM, outreach, white-label and resold local SEO and listings. GHL resells **Search Atlas** for SEO and heatmaps, and **Yext** for listings. Vendasta sells its own Local SEO plus Yext-powered Listing Sync Pro, alongside 250+ marketplace products. DashClicks pairs cheap white-label software with done-for-you fulfillment. AgencyAnalytics is reporting only. Lead finders (Outscraper, Scrap.io, D7) are cheap raw-data tools with no audit or pitch.

## Q2. How do agencies find, audit, pitch and onboard local clients, and which tool covers which step?

### Takeaway
The typical stack is: a scraper or lead finder (Outscraper, Scrap.io, D7, Apify) for **find**; an audit generator (Vendasta Snapshot, DashClicks InstaReports, GHL Prospecting, SEOptimer embed) for **audit**; a CRM with email and SMS (GHL, Vendasta) for **pitch**; and a sub-account plus resold products (listings, SEO, reputation) for **onboard and fulfill**. Reporting (AgencyAnalytics) is often bought separately.

### Inferences
- Agencies today stitch together 3 to 5 tools. The handoff from "audit" to "fulfillment plan" is manual. No audit I found becomes the client's task list after the sale (inference; none of the vendor pages describe that continuity).
- The existing audits are presence audits: listings, reviews, social, site speed, ads. Only GHL Premium and Leadsnap show geo-grid map rank, and none of the pages I read describe a **missed-revenue** estimate or an AI-written pitch grounded in grid data (inference from the feature lists read).

- I found no first-party description of the contents of GHL's AI-recommended outreach templates.

## Q3. Does any tool unify prospecting (find + score + enrich + audit + pitch) with fulfillment (rank tracking, fixes, reporting)? Who comes closest?

### Takeaway
**GoHighLevel comes closest.** Premium Prospecting gives AI lead discovery by niche and area, an audit with competitor heatmaps, a CRM, email and SMS. Fulfillment comes from Search Atlas (OTTO automated fixes, heatmaps, rank tracking) at $79 per sub-account and Yext listings at $30. Even so, the pieces are separately priced resold modules. There is no ease-of-sale scoring or contact enrichment of owners and line types documented, and no audit-to-task continuity. Vendasta is second: strong audit, CRM and fulfillment catalog, but no lead finder.

### Cited Findings
- **GHL covers find, audit and pitch:**
  - **Find:** AI prospecting by "target area and niche", delivered every few days.
  - **Audit:** heatmaps showing the top competitors per pin, plus website and SEO checks.
  - **Pitch:** push to CRM and workflows, then email and SMS.
  - **Not documented:** enrichment or owner identification — [GHL help](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005458-free-vs-premium-prospecting-in-gohighlevel-complete-guide-for-agencies)
- **GHL fulfillment via Search Atlas:** OTTO "audits, optimizes, and automates SEO fixes", plus local heatmaps and 1,000 tracked keywords — [Search Atlas release](https://searchatlas.com/blog/search-atlas-and-highlevel-bring-ai-powered-seo-to-1-and-a-half-million-businesses/). The heatmap quota of 1,000 points is shared across run, refresh and prefetch — [GHL help SEO](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004723-a-quick-walkthrough-of-seo-a-guide-to-boosting-your-search-rankings)
- **Vendasta:** the Snapshot audit, CRM, Yesware outreach with 100–300 enrichment credits, and Local SEO Pro with Grid, Listing Sync and Reputation AI. It has no native finder — [Vendasta pricing](https://www.vendasta.com/pricing/); [roadmap](https://roadmap.vendasta.com/ai-workforce/p/ai-workforce-automation-capability-skill-request-lead-finder-crm-integration-wit)
- **DashClicks:** audit (InstaReports) plus done-for-you fulfillment, but no discovery and no published rank tracking — [InstaReports](https://www.dashclicks.com/software/instareports); [GBP Ranker](https://www.dashclicks.com/services/gbp-ranker)
- **Leadsnap:** heatmaps of any business (usable as a sales audit), GBP management, white-label and a CRM, but no lead finder — [Leadsnap pricing](https://leadsnap.com/pricing/)
- **Semrush:** pulled back from the agency OS play by sunsetting its Client Portal and CRM — [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1546-agency-growth-kit-sunsetting)

### Inferences
- **OpenMarko's differentiation** (inference, compared against the feature sets above):
  1. **Ease-of-sale scoring** at the find step.
  2. **Owner, line-type and email verification** enrichment.
  3. A sales audit that includes **missed revenue, a game plan and a pitch email**.
  4. **Continuity** from that audit into a To-do/autopilot with one-click WordPress fixes and geo-grid tracking.
- **The risk is GHL's distribution.** With 60k+ agency customers, any GHL prospecting upgrade reaches most of the market. OpenMarko likely needs a GHL integration (push prospects and audits to GHL contacts) rather than competing head-on as a CRM.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether GHL Prospecting identifies owners or verifies emails, or which data provider it uses.
- I could not verify whether Search Atlas inside GHL includes GBP posting and management.

## Q4. Prices and unit economics for agencies (cost per sub-account, resale margins)

### Takeaway
Platform cost per client is low: GHL at $297 with unlimited sub-accounts, AgencyAnalytics at $20 per client, DashClicks at $199 flat. The real per-client costs are resold modules: GHL listings at $30 (suggested retail $45, about a 50% markup), GHL SEO at $79, GHL Premium Prospecting at $29, Leadsnap at about $3.58 per location, and Merchynt Paige at about $99 per location. ## Q5. Complaints and gaps

### Takeaway
Agency OS complaints center on complexity and learning curve (GHL), cost creep and confusing wholesale/retail pricing (Vendasta), and fulfillment quality and QA (DashClicks, Vendasta). Product gaps include AgencyAnalytics' weak map-pack tracking, the absence of a native lead finder at Vendasta, Semrush's retreat from agency CRM, and hand-offs between finding, auditing and fulfilling clients that remain manual.

### Cited Findings
- **GHL:**
  - **Learning curve:** 2 to 4 weeks to become comfortable — [G2](https://g2.com/products/highlevel/reviews?page=11)
  - **Product quality:** bugs and UI inconsistencies — [schedulingkit](https://schedulingkit.com/pros-and-cons/gohighlevel-pros-and-cons)
  - **Listings:** updates go through support tickets — [GHL listings](https://mp.gohighlevel.com/listings-management); agencies complained about needing client GBP access to sync — [GHL ideas](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/yext/p/more-of-the-yext-functionality-directly-in-ghl-for-customers)
  - **Heatmaps:** quota points are used on every refresh and prefetch — [GHL help SEO](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004723-a-quick-walkthrough-of-seo-a-guide-to-boosting-your-search-rankings)
- **Vendasta:**
  - **Cost:** it "can easily turn into one of an agency's most expensive outsourced costs" — [GetApp UK](https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2051052/vendasta)
  - **Pricing clarity:** wholesale-versus-retail pricing is confusing; some reviewers report undelivered content — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/168614/Vendasta-Platform/)
  - **Contracts:** one-year contracts above Starter, and minimum spend does not roll over — [Vendasta pricing](https://www.vendasta.com/pricing/)
- **DashClicks:**
  - **Fulfillment team:** slow to respond to fixes — [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/reviews/179692/dashclicks)
  - **Deliverable quality:** "no quality assurance" — [G2](https://g2.com/products/dashclicks/reviews/dashclicks-review-7501385)
- **AgencyAnalytics:**
  - **Map pack:** the 3-pack is not in Google Local tracking — [AA help](https://help.agencyanalytics.com/en/articles/4914282-what-is-google-local-in-the-rank-tracker)
  - **Pack vs. organic:** a forum user says it does not separate the local pack from organic the way BrightLocal and Whitespark do (user opinion, possibly dated) — [Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/looking-for-some-crowdsourcing-help-with-agency-analytics.56141/latest)
- **Semrush:** the Client Portal and CRM were sunset — [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1546-agency-growth-kit-sunsetting)
- **Lead finders:**
  - **Outscraper:** no owner names or line type in Maps and contacts — [Outscraper](https://outscraper.com/pricing/)
  - **D7:** paid pricing inconsistently published — [thegtmdirectory](https://thegtmdirectory.com/tools/d7-lead-finder)

### Inferences
- **Openings for OpenMarko:**
  - Audits that prove map-pack opportunity with geo-grids and missed revenue, versus generic A–F presence grades.
  - Owner-level enrichment.
  - Self-serve fixes (one-click WordPress) instead of opaque done-for-you fulfillment.
  - Transparent per-location pricing instead of wholesale minimums.
- **Integration as a wedge:** GHL is the de facto agency CRM, so a GHL integration is likely more valuable than building a CRM.

### Gaps
- Review data is largely aggregator summaries. I did not pull Reddit or agency-forum threads.
- Review counts and dates were not systematically sampled.
- I found no churn or retention data for any platform.
