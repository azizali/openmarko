# Enterprise & SMB Local Presence / Reputation Platforms (competitors to OpenMarko), as of Oct 2026

Scope: Yext, SOCi, Birdeye, Podium, Synup, Uberall (incl. MomentFeed), Thryv, Scorpion, plus Reputation (Reputation.com), Rio SEO, Chatmeter, LOCALiQ. Research date 2026-10-30. Third-party pricing aggregators (Costbench, SocialPilot, Pabau, Software Advice, etc.) are labeled as such. Several are competitors with a commercial interest, so treat them as indicative only.

## Q1. Which jobs overlap OpenMarko, and which don't?

### Takeaway
All of these platforms do listings sync, review monitoring with AI replies, and GBP/social posting, so they overlap with OpenMarko's review-reply and GBP-post features. In 2025–26 most added "AI search visibility" (GEO) and agentic automation. Geo-grid rank tracking has stopped being a gap: Synup includes a "Local Rank Grid" on every plan, and Yext bought Places Scout (hyper-local rank and competitor intelligence, now "Scout"). None of the vendors researched offers site audits with one-click WordPress fixes or agency prospecting/sales audits. Their center of gravity is listings distribution plus reviews, messaging and payments for multi-location brands.

### Inferences
- **Overlaps with OpenMarko:** review replies with AI, GBP/social posts, listings or insights, reporting, competitor benchmarking (Yext Scout, Birdeye Competitors, Synup), geo-grid rank tracking (Synup natively; Yext via Places Scout, but enterprise-priced), and "to-do / autopilot" (SOCi Genius Agents, Yext Action Center, Uberall UB-I, Birdeye BirdAI, Synup Sydekick all market agentic execution).
- **Gaps in the incumbents (none found):** website technical audit with one-click WordPress fixes; agency prospecting/sales-audit tooling. Synup's white-label agency plans come closest to the agency use case.
- **Things they do that OpenMarko doesn't:** a direct publisher network or listings distribution (Yext 200+, Uberall, Synup); messaging, webchat and payments (Podium, Birdeye); CRM and scheduling (Thryv, Birdeye Myna); managed ads and websites (Scorpion, LOCALiQ); surveys and ticketing (Birdeye Robin).
- Synup is the most direct product competitor: geo-grid, AI replies, agency white label, $49–$899/month.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm Places Scout uses a literal geo-grid methodology inside Yext Scout today (the search snippets did not say).
- Could not verify Uberall, SOCi or Birdeye geo-grid rank-tracking features. Not found in sources reviewed.
- Podium's current feature list (2026) was not fetched in detail.

## Q2. Price points: single location/SMB vs enterprise

### Takeaway
Most of these vendors hide pricing. Third-party reports put Birdeye at about $299–$449/location/month and Podium at about $399–$599/month on annual contracts. Yext self-serve runs about $199–$999/location/year, while Vendr reports a median enterprise ACV around $318K. SOCi deals reportedly start around $15K/year. Thryv bundles reach $1,475+/month, and Scorpion managed marketing runs $1.5K–$10K+/month. Synup is the only vendor with fully public, month-to-month pricing: $49 for 1 location up to $899 for 50.

### Cited Findings
- **Synup (official):**
  - Plans: Solo $49/mo for 1 location; Premium $299 for up to 10; Pro $499 for up to 25; Scale $899 for up to 50; Enterprise custom.
  - Annual billing saves 20%. Plans are monthly and cancellable at the end of the period. There is a 14-day trial.
  - Credits meter agent work. — [Synup pricing](https://www.synup.com/pricing)
- **Yext:**
  - Self-serve tiers reported at Emerging $199, Essential $449, Complete $499 and Premium $999 per location per year (as of Aug 2026, third-party). — [Spike blog](https://getspike.ai/blog/yext-pricing/)
  - Vendr data (via a third party) gives a median ACV of $318,204, a range of $23,767–$534,000, and 3–7% annual escalators. — [Spike blog](https://getspike.ai/blog/yext-pricing/)
  - The 10-K says annual and multi-year subscriptions are priced by feature package and number of "licenses" (locations, persons, events). Capacity-based pricing was introduced for Pages and Search. — [Yext FY26 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001614178/000162828026027494/fy26arsform10-k.htm)
  - 92% of ARR comes from customers paying $50K+ per year. — [Yext Q2 FY27 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001614178/000162828026059706/ex992q2fy27shareholderlett.htm)
- **Birdeye:**
  - The official page shows no prices. Quotes are by location tier (1–3, 4–9, 10–39, 40–249, 250–999, 1000+). — [Birdeye pricing](https://birdeye.com/pricing/)
  - Third-party reports: Starter about $299, Growth about $349 (one source says $399), Dominate about $449 (one says $499) per location per month. Premium is custom for 4+ locations. Contracts are 12-month, billed annually, with no free trial and onboarding fees of $500–$1,500 per site. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=721229); [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/review-management/birdeye)
- **Podium:**
  - The official page shows no prices; the AI Employee is an add-on. — [Podium pricing](https://www.podium.com/pricing)
  - Third-party reports: Core $399/mo, Pro $599/mo (possibly per location), Signature custom, all on annual contracts. SMS overage is billed on credits, plus a $5/location/month low-volume registration fee. — [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/podium-pricing/); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/reviews/podium-pricing); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/164285/Podium/pricing/)
- **SOCi:**
  - Contact-us pricing. Annual flexible pricing; "most partnerships start around $15,000 per year". — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/localiq-vs-soci); [Software Advice AU](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/87719/soci)
  - An AWS Marketplace listing shows $30/unit/location MRR with a 100-location minimum. It is unclear whether that matches standard deals. — [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-6lwtxn7b65dcu)
- **Thryv:**
  - The official page shows packages bundling Marketing Center Pro, Reporting, Keap or Business Center Pro, and a Growth Package. The top "Accelerate" tier starts at $1,475/mo. — [Thryv pricing](https://www.thryv.com/?p=94393)
  - Third-party figures: Kickstart $623, Ignite $929, Growth $1,133/mo; Marketing Center about $255/mo. These conflict with the official page. — [Software Advice](https://softwareadvice.com/crm/thryv-profile/)
  - Thryv defines "quality customers" as those paying more than $400 MRR, about 72% of SaaS revenue. — [Quartr Q2 2026](https://quartr.com/events/thryv-holdings-inc-thry-q2-2026_3eRD9HUz)
- **Scorpion:**
  - No public pricing. Its own FAQ cites a typical 12-month contract for technology and SEO. — [Scorpion FAQ](https://www.scorpion.co/why-scorpion/faqs)
  - Competitor estimates: $1,500–$10,000+/mo plus ad spend. — [Rebolt (competitor)](https://rebolthq.com/alternatives/scorpion-alternatives)
  - A Trustpilot reviewer reports paying $10K/mo. — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/scorpion.co)

### Inferences
- A single-location SMB pays about $50/mo (Synup Solo), about $17–$83/mo (Yext self-serve, annualized), or about $300–$600/mo (Birdeye or Podium, annual contract). Thryv runs about $600–$1,500/mo and Scorpion $1.5K–$10K/mo for done-for-you work.
- Enterprise brands pay roughly $15K–$500K+/year (SOCi entry about $15K; Yext median ACV about $318K).
- This leaves a pricing gap between Synup/BrightLocal-class tools and the $300+/location/month reputation suites. Single-location SMBs and agencies have a lot of room under Birdeye and Podium price points.

### Gaps
- No official list prices for Birdeye, Podium, SOCi, Uberall, Reputation, Rio SEO, Chatmeter or Scorpion.
- Uberall pricing: not found.
- The Yext self-serve tier figures come from a single third-party blog; yext.com/pricing returned 404.
- The Vendr ACV figure is second-hand (cited via Spike, not Vendr directly).

## Q4. Weaknesses and customer complaints

### Takeaway
The dominant complaints are annual auto-renewing contracts with long notice windows (Birdeye, Podium, Scorpion), high per-location cost, and lock-in:
- Yext listings may revert after cancellation (disputed).
- Scorpion clients have disputed website ownership.
- Podium's review ratings are strong overall but negative specifically on contract termination.

### Cited Findings
- **Yext lock-in:**
  - Yext's FAQ says cancelling does not overwrite updates or force a revert, but it stops sending data to publishers. — [Yext FAQ](https://www.yext.com/knowledge-center/yext-faq)
  - BrightLocal (a competitor) says some listings remain, some disappear and some revert. — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/?p=114769)
  - Whitespark sells a "Yext replacement service". — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/yext-replacement-service/)
  - Yext's own data shows churn in small accounts (sub-$50K GRR 69%). — [Q2 FY27 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001614178/000162828026059706/ex992q2fy27shareholderlett.htm)
- **Podium:**
  - Not BBB-accredited; 65 complaints in 3 years. — [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/lehi/profile/computer-software-developers/podium-1166-90023083/complaints)
  - Reviews cite auto-renewal for a full year, ignored cancellations and billing after cancellation. — [ProductReview.com.au](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/podium); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/164285/Podium/reviews/?page=3); [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/podium.com?page=9)

### Inferences
- OpenMarko can position on transparent, month-to-month pricing with no listings hostage (it does not depend on a publisher-network subscription), and on SEO depth (geo-grid, audits, WordPress fixes) that the reputation suites lack.
- Synup already uses the month-to-month positioning, so it is the benchmark to beat.

### Gaps
- No systematic review analysis for Uberall, Reputation, Rio SEO, Chatmeter or Synup.
- The complaint evidence is anecdotal user reviews; the BBB count is the only quantitative figure.
