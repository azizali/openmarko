# All-in-one local SEO software suites (competitors to OpenMarko) — as of Oct 30, 2026

Scope: BrightLocal, Whitespark, Semrush Local, Moz Local, SE Ranking (Local Marketing add-on), Surfer, Ahrefs. Also noted as significant: Local Falcon, Synup, GatherUp, Localo. Vendor pricing pages fetched Oct 30, 2026 unless stated otherwise. moz.com blocks our fetcher and search tool, so Moz figures come from third parties (dated below). Research budget limits mean some company and build facts are gaps. See each Gaps section.

## Q1. How deep is each suite's local feature set compared with OpenMarko?

### Takeaway
BrightLocal and Semrush Local are the closest all-in-one matches. Each has geo-grid tracking, listings, reviews, GBP posting and reporting, and Semrush now pitches an autonomous "GBP AI Agent." Whitespark is a set of separate best-in-class tools plus managed services. Moz Local is listings- and reviews-first. SE Ranking bundles basic local tracking and listings into a general SEO suite. Ahrefs and Surfer have no meaningful local suite. None of the sources reviewed show one-click WordPress fixes, a to-do/autopilot fix queue tied to a site audit, or a prospecting-to-sales-audit workflow comparable to OpenMarko's. Local Falcon is the main competitor in sales and prospecting enablement around geo-grids.

### Cited Findings
**BrightLocal**
- Track plan: Local AI Visibility Tracker, local SEO audits, rank monitoring for up to 100 keywords and 4 competitors, geo-grid maps for up to 5 keywords, citation accuracy monitoring, GBP audits — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Manage plan adds "AI Insights," Active Sync for key sites, and GBP post scheduling. Grow adds multi-site review monitoring and response, review requests by email, SMS and in-store, and review widgets for your website — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- The comparison table also lists Local Search Grid, Local Rank Tracker, white-labelling, a GA integration and API access. Plan-level allocation was not shown in the fetched text — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Citation Builder submits to 5 aggregators: Data Axle, Neustar Localeze, Foursquare, Yellow Pages Network and GPS Network. Coverage is US, UK, Canada and Australia. Free tools include Local Search Results Checker, Local RankFlux and Review Link Generator — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Responding to reviews from inside the dashboard works only for Google and Facebook (G2 reviewer, quoted secondhand) — [Birdeye summary of G2 reviews](https://birdeye.com/au/tools/brightlocal-reviews/)

**Semrush Local**
- Base ($30/location/month): GBP AI Agent that creates and publishes posts, replies to reviews automatically, posts photos and optimizes descriptions; GBP audit and optimization plan; competitor heatmap reports; Map Rank Tracker geo-grid with 375 credits; Map Rank Tracker reporting API — [Semrush KB 1611](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans); [Semrush Local pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/)
- Pro ($60/location/month) adds Listing Management (pushes to "70+ directories with just 1 click," plus WebMD, Vitals and Wellness.com for US healthcare), review management with AI reply drafts, SMS and email review campaigns, advanced GBP optimization, and 1,225 Map Rank Tracker credits — [Semrush KB 1611](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans)
- Reporting add-ons: Base Report $10/month (GA/GSC, PDF). Pro Report $20/month (white-label, AI summaries, share by link) — [Semrush Local pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/)
- A third-party guide puts listing reach at "150+ directories," which conflicts with Semrush's own "70+" — [aionx review](https://aionx.co/ai-comparisons/semrush-local-seo-toolkit-review/) vs [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans)
- One reviewer says Map Rank Tracker lacks a visual heatmap. Semrush's own docs describe competitor heatmap reports, so treat that claim as outdated or wrong — [aionx](https://aionx.co/ai-comparisons/semrush-local-seo-toolkit-review/); [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans)

**Whitespark**
- Products are sold separately rather than bundled. Local Platform is a GBP management tool ($1/location/month) that blocks unwanted GBP edits and supports bulk edits. Local Ranking Grids track up to 225 grid points. Also: Local Rank Tracker (traditional), Local Citation Finder, Reputation Builder (review requests and management), Listings Service, a Yext Replacement Service, and a GBP Reinstatement Service — [Whitespark pricing](https://whitespark.ca/pricing/)
- Citation Finder finds and tracks citations but does not submit them. Submission goes through the one-time Listings Service — [Dupple, Oct 23 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)

**Moz Local** (third-party sources only; moz.com was unreachable)
- Lite: directory management, Local Grid and review monitoring. Preferred adds review responding and social posting to Google and Facebook, and review generation starts at this tier. Listings cover only the US, UK and Canada. AI is sold as add-ons: Listings AI and Reviews AI — [Dupple, Oct 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Sources conflict on AI review replies: one SocialPilot piece says an AI assistant drafts replies, another says there are no automated AI responses — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=712380); [SocialPilot review](https://www.socialpilot.co/reviews/tools/moz-local)
- Distribution historically went through aggregators (Infogroup, Localeze, Factual, etc.), some of which overlap with Yext's network. This is old forum data, likely pre-2020 — [Moz Q&A forum](https://mozdev.nodebb.com/community/q/post/49684)

**SE Ranking**
- Local Marketing add-on: "Local & Map Rank Tracker" (1,500 keywords), listing management across directories in 100+ countries, and GBP posts. Core and Growth plans each include 3 locations. White-label requires the Agency Pack add-on — [SE Ranking subscription page](https://seranking.com/subscription.html)
- The fetched pricing page did not mention geo-grid, review management or local reports — [SE Ranking subscription page](https://seranking.com/subscription.html)

**Ahrefs / Surfer**
- Ahrefs is not a dedicated local suite. Rank Tracker targets by country, city and device, with no geo-grid — [cloro.dev roundup](https://cloro.dev/blog/local-seo-tools/)
- No "Surfer Local" product turned up. Surfer appears in roundups only as an AI/content SEO tool — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools); [cloro.dev](https://cloro.dev/blog/local-seo-tools/)

**Other significant suites**
- Local Falcon: a geo-grid specialist priced per credit (1 credit = 1 pin). Adds Falcon AI reports (25 credits each), a data API, a Claude Connector, and, per its pages, "Falcon Agent" and "Sales Enablement" products (prices not retrieved) — [Local Falcon KB pricing](https://www.localfalcon.com/knowledge-base/kb81-how-much-does-local-falcon-cost-a-complete-guide-to-local-falcons-pricing); [Local Falcon pricing](https://localfalcon.com/pricing)
- Synup: listings distribution, review campaigns and a Local Rank Grid on all plans. GatherUp: reviews plus Listings Hub (125 directories), no rank grid. Localo: position map, reviews inbox and citations manager, but it tracks listings rather than pushing them — [Dupple, Oct 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)

### Inferences
- Semrush's GBP AI Agent (auto-posting, auto-replies, photo posting, description optimization) is the closest match to OpenMarko's "autopilot." None of the sources show it fixing websites.
- OpenMarko's per-competitor geo-grid maps are roughly matched by Semrush's competitor heatmap reports and BrightLocal's tracking of 4 competitors. Nobody in this set is shown bundling geo-grid with lead prospecting and per-prospect sales audits; Local Falcon's Sales Enablement product comes closest. That is an inference; the details were not retrieved.
- None of the vendor pages reviewed mention GBP performance insights (calls, directions). Not seeing it doesn't prove the feature is absent.

### Gaps
- How each vendor gets its data: no source confirmed whether BrightLocal, Semrush, SE Ranking or Whitespark use their own SERP crawlers or DataForSEO-type providers. No Yext partnership for Semrush Listing Management was found, and Semrush's KB does not name a partner.
- Moz Local's current feature page and its listing partners could not be fetched (moz.com is blocked).
- Whether BrightLocal, Semrush or Moz expose GBP performance metrics (calls, directions) was not verified.
- Grid sizes and per-scan credit costs for Semrush Map Rank Tracker are not published on the KB page.

## Q2. What does each suite charge for local features, per location per month?

### Takeaway
At one location the main suites cost about $20–$65/month: Moz Local $20–$40, Semrush Local $30–$60 (annual billing), BrightLocal $41–$65 monthly or $31–$49 annual-equivalent. Citations and managed services are extra. Whitespark sells à la carte, so a full stack costs roughly $128/month. Geo-grid-only tools charge per grid pin: Local Falcon credits cost $0.05 each.

### Cited Findings
**BrightLocal** (official, Oct 2026; increase effective July 1, 2026 per third parties)
- 1 location: Track $41/month or $369/year ($31/month); Manage $54 or $485 ($40/month); Grow $65 or $584 ($49/month) — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Location bands, monthly price (Track / Manage / Grow):
  - 2–5 locations: $62 / $76 / $87
  - 6–10 locations: $83 / $109 / $131
  - 11–20 locations: $104 / $153 / $197
  - 41–50 locations: $261 / $384 / $494
  - 91–100 locations: $524 / $769 / $989
  - 100+ locations: quote
- At 100 locations, Grow works out to about $9.89/location/month billed monthly (inference from the table) — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Annual billing saves 25%. Trial is 14 days with no card — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- Add-ons: Citation Builder from $2/citation (FAQ: $3.20/site, $2 with bulk credits). Managed SEO from $499/month. API pricing is custom — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- A third party says the price increase took effect July 2026 — [Stork](https://www.stork.ai/en/brightlocal); [CostBench changelog Sept 2026](https://costbench.com/changelog/brightlocal-pricing-update-2026-09/)

**Semrush Local** (official)
- Plans, billed annually:
  - Base: $30/location/month
  - Pro: $60/location/month
  - Business: custom, for 20+ locations
- Add-ons:
  - Extra users: $20/month

- Sources: [Semrush Local pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/); [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans)
- Local is sold as a toolkit separate from Semrush's core SEO plans — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Older pricing (Basic $20 / Premium $40, or Essentials $50 / Advanced $60) is out of date — [WebSearch summary of third-party sites, e.g. toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/semrush/pricing)

**Whitespark** (official)
- Local Platform (GBP management): $1/location/month
- Local Ranking Grids: from $10/month (Dupple: 5x5 grid = 25 credits)
- Local Rank Tracker: $14–$200/month
- Local Citation Finder: $33–$149/month
- Reputation Builder: $79/location/month
- SEO Services: $499–$1,999/month

- Sources: [Whitespark pricing](https://whitespark.ca/pricing/); [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Citation Finder + Grids + Reputation Builder together cost about $128/month — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)

**Moz Local** (third party, prices read Oct 20, 2026)
- Per location: Lite $20/month or $199/year; Preferred $30 or $299; Elite $40 or $399
- AI add-ons: Listings AI +$14/month; Reviews AI +$10/month
- Sources: [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools); matches [CostBench, verified Jun 13 2026](https://www.costbench.com/software/seo-tools/moz-local/)

**SE Ranking** (official)
- Core: $129/month ($103.20 annual). Growth: $279/month ($223.20 annual). Both include 3 locations.
- Extra locations "start from $18.40." The page doesn't say per what period.
- Agency Pack (white-label): +$69/month, annual billing only.
- Source: [SE Ranking subscription](https://seranking.com/subscription.html)
- Third-party figures conflict (about $7.50/location; €173/month for 50 locations) — [marcandrews.com](https://marcandrews.com/?p=3713); [journaldugeek](https://www.journaldugeek.com/seo/outils/se-ranking/prix/)

**Local Falcon** (vendor KB)
- Monthly: $24.99 (7,500 credits) to $199.99 (63,000 credits). Annual: $299.88 to $2,399.88.
- Pay-as-you-go: $0.05/credit. Enterprise: $499–$4,999/month.
- A 7x7 scan costs 49 credits. Monthly credits expire; annual credits roll over.
- Source: [Local Falcon KB](https://www.localfalcon.com/knowledge-base/kb81-how-much-does-local-falcon-cost-a-complete-guide-to-local-falcons-pricing)

**Others**
- Synup Solo: $49/month or $39 annual (1 location)
- GatherUp: $60/location annual or $75 monthly (multi-location); Listings Hub +$40/location/month

- Source: [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)

### Inferences
- The going rate for a single-location local suite clusters at about $30–$60/month. That sets the anchor for OpenMarko's SMB pricing.
- BrightLocal's banded pricing becomes very cheap per location at agency scale (about $5–$10/location at 91–100 locations). Semrush's flat per-location fee does not. That makes BrightLocal the price-sensitive agency benchmark.

### Gaps
- Moz Local prices are not verified on moz.com.
- SE Ranking's per-location add-on billing period is not confirmed.
- Semrush per-scan credit costs are not published.

## Q3. Who are their customers, and how big are the companies?

### Takeaway
BrightLocal (about 15k customers, bootstrapped, Brighton UK) and Whitespark (claims 100k+ users, Edmonton, founded 2005) mainly serve SMBs and agencies. Semrush, now owned by Adobe (deal closed April 28, 2026; about $1.9B), had about $455M ARR and roughly 114k paying customers before the acquisition. Its Local toolkit targets SMBs and growing multi-location businesses. Moz Local belongs to Ziff Davis (not verified this session) and aims at multi-location listings management.

### Cited Findings
- BrightLocal: "15,000+ platform customers," 83,000+ newsletter subscribers, "trusted by businesses, agencies, and platforms for over 15 years" — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/)
- BrightLocal was founded in Brighton, England. Sources give 2008 or 2009, and co-founders as Myles Anderson (CEO) and Ed Eliot. Funding is unclear: one profile says it never raised, another says "Growth" stage — [BrightLocal about](https://brightlocal.com/about-us); [Craft](https://craft.co/brightlocal); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/brightlocal-vs-synup)
- Whitespark: founded 2005 in Edmonton, Alberta. Claims "over 100,000 enterprises, agencies, and small businesses globally" — [Whitespark pricing](https://whitespark.ca/pricing/)
- Semrush:
  - Adobe announced the deal in November 2025 (about $1.9B, all-cash) and completed it April 28, 2026 — [MarTech](https://martech.org/adobe-buys-semrush/); [Semrush customer FAQ](https://www.semrush.com/news/455963-faq-for-customers-adobe-acquires-semrush/); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/adobe-completes-acquisition-of-semrush-expanding-brand-visibility-and-ai-search-capabilities/amp/)
  - Semrush is being folded into Adobe's "CX Enterprise." Semrush Enterprise is now quote-only through the co-branded "Adobe Brand Visibility" offering — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/adobe-acquires-semrush-to-extend-brand-visibility-into-ai-search-and-agentic-discovery-within-cx-enterprise/); [Semrush Local pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/)
  - Q3 2025 (last filings before the deal): ARR $455.4M (+14% YoY), quarterly revenue $112.1M (+15%) — [Semrush Q3 2025 release](https://www.semrush.com/news/433598-semrush-announces-third-quarter-2025-financial-results/); [SEC-filed release PDF](https://s27.q4cdn.com/202248034/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/SEMR-Q3-2025-Earnings-Release.pdf)
  - About 114k paying customers (down from about 116k in Q2). Customers paying $10k+ grew 30%, $50k+ grew 72%. These figures come from secondary summaries — [MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2025-11-5-semrush-holdings-inc-stock-1/); [Fintool](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/SEMR/earnings/Q3%202025)
  - The Local customer story is Smileworks dental practice (a vendor claim) — [Semrush Local pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/)
- Semrush Local ICP: Base is "for small local businesses," Pro "for growing businesses," Business for 20+ locations — [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans)
- Moz Local is positioned for multi-location listings management — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/116352/Moz-Pro/)

### Inferences
- With Semrush inside Adobe, Semrush's focus is likely to shift toward enterprise and AI visibility. That could leave SMB local customers underserved. This is speculative.

### Gaps
- Semrush has no separate Local revenue or customer figures. Post-acquisition, Semrush no longer files with the SEC.
- Ziff Davis's ownership of Moz (acquired 2021, from training memory) and Moz's segment revenue were not verified this session.
- BrightLocal and Whitespark revenue, headcount and notable customers were not found.
- SE Ranking company facts (HQ, customers) were not researched within budget.

## Q4. What do users complain about?

### Takeaway
Complaints focus on cost stacking and credit math (Semrush), slow report and grid generation plus limited review-response scope (BrightLocal), Moz Local's weak posting features and support, and the à la carte cost and lack of automated submission at Whitespark. Support and ease of use earn consistent praise for BrightLocal.

### Cited Findings
- BrightLocal:
  - Ratings: G2 4.6 (about 223–228 reviews), Capterra 4.8 (281), Trustpilot 4.5–4.6 (752–753) — [BrightLocal pricing](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/); [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/brightlocal.com)
  - Cons: slow report generation and "time delays when generating grid results" (secondhand summary of G2) — [localdominator](https://localdominator.co/brightlocal-reviews/)
  - Review replies only for Google and Facebook — [Birdeye/G2 quote](https://birdeye.com/au/tools/brightlocal-reviews/)
  - Praise: support and an intuitive dashboard — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/brightlocal-vs-local-falcon)
- Semrush Local:
  - Costs stack: a base Semrush subscription ($139.95+) plus the Local add-on plus scan credits. "Credit-based pricing requires calculation" — [aionx](https://aionx.co/ai-comparisons/semrush-local-seo-toolkit-review/)
  - No free trial on the Local cards. Base lacks listings and review requests — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
  - Praise: listing management "a real time-saver" (G2 reviewer quoted secondhand) — [aionx](https://aionx.co/ai-comparisons/semrush-local-seo-toolkit-review/)
- Moz Local:
  - G2 rating 4.2 (475 reviews). Complaints about missing posting features, listing reductions and poor support — [G2 review](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/moz-local-review-3310587); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/retail-consumer-services-software/a/moz-local/reviews/)
  - Limited to the US, UK and Canada. AI features are paid add-ons — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Whitespark: separate products add up (about $128/month), and citation submission is a separate one-time service — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Local Falcon: covers only the rank grid, and monthly credits expire — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-local-seo-tools)
- Geo-grid accuracy: one tester reported 7 of 10 platforms were inaccurate enough to mislead. This is an anecdotal blog post — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/best-local-rank-tracker-2026-i-tracked-6-businesses-across-10-platforms-and-3-were-actually-accurate-7b8078e808)

### Inferences
- OpenMarko can position against credit math and fragmented à la carte tools with all-in pricing that includes geo-grid scans. Its "To-do with autopilot, plus website fixes" is a differentiator that none of these suites show.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/localseo or r/SEO threads were retrieved. Search returned none, so the sentiment above comes from aggregators and review sites.
- Ahrefs and SE Ranking local-specific complaints were not collected.
