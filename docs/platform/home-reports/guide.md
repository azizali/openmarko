# Home and reports: how to

## Read your weekly brief

**Home** shows the last 28 days (calls from Google, map pack, AI answers, how often your posts were seen), your **Presence score** broken down by area, what's happening on social this week, what changed while you were away, and the three things worth doing this week. Ask the assistant anything from the box at the bottom.

## Send a report

1. Open **Reports** and choose **New report**, or open an existing one.
2. Pick the sections, who gets it and how often.
3. The preview shows exactly what your client sees. Switch keyword progress between Google search and the map pack.
4. Download a PDF any time.
