# To-do, Autopilot and Schedules: how to

## Work through your To-do

**To-do** is the one list of things worth doing: fixes, review replies, articles and more, highest impact first.

1. Filter by type or keyword.
2. Open a task to see why it matters, the effort, how it's applied, and the exact change (you can edit it).
3. Choose **Apply**, **Mark done** (if you did it yourself) or **Dismiss**. Applied changes can be reverted.
4. Select several and apply them together.

Applying something anywhere (here, on Home, or from the assistant) marks it done everywhere.

## Decide what the assistant does on its own

**Settings → Autopilot** lists each kind of change the assistant can make, with its risk.

- **Off**: never.
- **Ask first**: it goes in your To-do for approval.
- **On its own**: done for you, logged in Activity with Undo.

Start from a preset (Careful, Low risk on its own, Hands-off) and adjust. Some things, like replies to 1–3★ reviews, are always seen by a person first.

## Change when things run

**Settings → Schedules** lists every scan, sync and review with how often it runs and when it last and next runs. Change the frequency, pause it, or choose **Run now**.

## See what ran

**Settings → Activity** logs every scan, crawl, sync and search with status, progress, how long it took, what started it and what it cost.
