# Getting around: how to

## Find anything

Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows). Type a page, setting or action. If nothing matches, or you type a sentence, send it to the assistant from the same box.

## Pick a business and location

The switcher at the top of the sidebar picks the business and, with several locations, **All locations** or one. The bar under a page title switches location without leaving the page. With **All locations**, location pages show a comparison table; choose **Open** on a row.

## When a page says Connect

Some pages need something set up first: a Google profile, a website, or a social account. The page says what's missing and links to it. Everything else keeps working without it.

## Refresh data

Each page's toolbar shows when its data was last updated and has a button to run it now (Run scan, Re-crawl site…). While it's running, a live chip shows progress.

## Dark mode

The moon at the bottom of the sidebar (top bar on phones), or **Settings → Appearance**.

## Your own settings

**Settings → Profile, Appearance, Notifications, Plan & billing** belong to you and follow you to every business you open.
