# Your brand on the app: how to

For agency admins. Your clients see your brand instead of ours everywhere: the app, reports, shared audits, emails and the assistant.

## Set your brand

1. Open **Settings → Whitelabel** and make sure it's on.
2. Enter the app name (blank uses your agency name) and the assistant's name (blank uses "AI Assistant").
3. Upload a wide logo and a square icon, and pick an accent colour. Changes show right away.
4. Add your support link so "Contact support" goes to you.

## Use your own domain

1. Under **Domain**, enter something like `app.youragency.com` and choose **Connect domain**.
2. Add the record shown where your domain is hosted.
3. Choose **Check DNS**. Once found, it shows **Live · SSL**.

The fallback address always works with your brand, even if your domain's DNS breaks.

## Send emails from your address

Under **Emails**, set the sender name and from address, add the DNS records shown, and choose **Verify**. Until it's verified, emails come from your fallback address.
