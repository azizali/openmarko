# Organizations, locations and websites: how to

## Switch business

Use the switcher at the top of the sidebar. It lists every business you have access to. With several locations, it also picks **All locations** or one location.

## Add a business

Choose **Add organization** in the switcher (or search for it with ⌘K). This starts setup: enter the website or business name and the app finds the rest. See the onboarding guide.

## Add or edit a location

1. **Settings → Locations → Add location**.
2. Enter the name and address, and pick which website serves it.
3. Connect its Google Business Profile from the location's row.

Pages about one place (Business Profile, Reviews, Rankings, Citations, Competitors, AI answers) follow the location you pick in the switcher or in the bar under the page title.

## Add a website

1. **Settings → Websites → Add website**.
2. Paste the address and pick the locations it serves.
3. Connect Search Console, Google Analytics and WordPress for it.

Site health, Articles and Backlinks show the website serving the chosen location.

## For agencies

- **Settings → Clients** lists every business you manage. They all use your brand.
- **Transfer out** hands a business back: it keeps all its data and its own team, stops seeing your brand, and your staff lose access. This can't be undone; they'd have to invite you back.
