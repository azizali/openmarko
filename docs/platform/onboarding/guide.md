# Adding a business: how to

1. Choose **Add organization** in the business switcher (or find it with ⌘K).
2. Enter the business's website or name. The app reads its website, Google Business Profiles, social links and reviews.
3. Check **What we found**: the business, its locations, websites and social accounts. Fix anything that's wrong, and say which location each account speaks for.
4. **Connect** Google and social accounts, or skip any of them and connect later in Settings → Integrations.
5. Review the drafted **Brand voice**.
6. Under **What to track**, untick anything you don't want and add your own keywords, competitors and profiles to watch. Set the map-scan grid and how often it runs.
7. Choose **Launch**. The business appears in the switcher.
