# Team and roles: how to

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Choose **Invite**, enter their email and pick a role. The description under the role says what it allows.
3. Choose **Send invite**. They show as **Invited** until they join.

To cancel an invite, use the bin icon on their row.

## Change what someone can do

On **Settings → Team → People**, pick a new role next to their name. It takes effect right away. Each person has exactly one role; to give someone different access, give them a different role.

The Owner's role can't be changed here.

## Make a custom role

1. Open **Settings → Team → Roles** and choose **New role**, or open a built-in role and choose **Duplicate**.
2. Name it and describe what the person does.
3. Set **Everything**: the starting level for every area.
4. Set a level per area (Local SEO, Social media, Ads, Customers, Prospecting, Assistant, Organization). **Same** follows the level above.
5. Open an area to set one feature differently, or choose **Pick actions…** to tick exact actions, for example "Draft replies" without "Post replies".
6. **Create role**.

Levels:

| Level | Means |
|---|---|
| None | Hidden |
| View | See it, change nothing |
| Edit | Draft and change; nothing goes out |
| Publish | Also post, send and spend |
| Full | Also connect, delete and export |

Features added later follow their area's level, so you don't need to update roles when the app grows.

## Check what a role sees

On **Settings → Team → Roles**, choose **Preview** on a role. The sidebar, pages and buttons show exactly what that role can do. Choose **Stop preview** in the bar at the top to return.

## Delete a custom role

Open the role and choose **Delete**. Anyone who had it becomes a Viewer.

## For agencies: your own staff

**Settings → Partner team** (agency admins only):

- **Invite** adds someone as a Member with no clients.
- **Clients** on a Member's row picks which client businesses they can open, and their role on each.
- Switching someone to **Admin** gives them Admin on every client.
- Removing someone removes them from every client.

A client's own Team page lists your staff who can open it, but only your agency manages them.
