# Rankings & competitors: how to

## Check where you rank

Open **Rankings**. Each keyword shows your Google position and your map pack position, with a small map of where you show up.

- Switch between **All results**, **Organic** and **Map pack**.
- Filter to keywords that improved, declined, or are in the top 3.
- Open a keyword to see the full map: tap any point to see who ranks there, change the grid size, and compare now with 30 days ago.

Choose **Run scan** to refresh now; otherwise scans run on the schedule in Settings → Schedules.

## Compare with competitors

**Competitors** lists the businesses that share the map pack with you. Open one for a head-to-head: the gaps to close, their categories, and which keywords you both rank for.

## See what's holding you back

**Ranking factors** scores each area Google weighs (profile, reviews, on-page, links, citations, behaviour). Choose **Fix** to jump to the task in To-do.

## Find new keywords

**Keyword ideas** suggests related searches, questions, keywords competitors rank for and "near me" searches. Select ones you like and choose **Track**. **Generate more ideas** adds another batch.
