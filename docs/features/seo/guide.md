# Business Profile: how to

## See how your Google profile is doing

Open **Business Profile**. Pick a location in the switcher if you have several. You'll see calls, direction requests and searches from Google over the last 90 days, and how complete your profile is.

## Improve your profile

Missing or weak details (hours, categories, services, description) show up as tasks in **To-do**. Open one to see the exact change, edit it if you like, and apply it.

## Connect a Google profile

If the page says **Connect**, the location has no Google profile linked yet. Connect it from the message, or from **Settings → Locations**.

Google posts are made in **Posts & calendar**; reviews are on the **Reviews** tab.
