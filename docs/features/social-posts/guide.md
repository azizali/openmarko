# Posts & calendar: how to

## Plan your week

Open **Posts & calendar**. The next 7 days show what's scheduled; drafts appear dashed. Lists below show everything scheduled, in draft, and published.

## Write a post

1. Choose **New post** (or start from a trend or swipe idea).
2. Pick the accounts it goes to. Each account gets its own version.
3. Adjust the tone (Shorter, More playful, More expert) or edit any version directly.
4. Check the preview: where the text gets cut off, how the image is cropped, character limits.
5. **Save draft**, **Schedule** (each platform shows its best time) or **Post now**.

## See how posts did

Published posts show views and reactions. The totals at the top break down by platform and say which accounts aren't connected yet.

You need at least one connected account (Settings → Integrations → Social, or a Google Business Profile).
