# The assistant: how to

## Ask anything

Press **⌘J** (Ctrl+J), choose the assistant in the sidebar, or tap the floating button (drag it anywhere). It opens beside the page you're on and knows what you're looking at, so you can ask "why did this drop?" without explaining.

You can also type a question into **⌘K** and send it from there.

## Let it fix things

When an answer includes changes, each one has **Apply** and **Undo**, just like To-do. Say "do all" or "fix them" to apply everything from the last answer. Anything that needs a person is listed with steps instead. What it may do without asking is set in **Settings → Autopilot**.

## Write posts

Ask "What's trending this week?" or "Write an Instagram post about the time change". It drafts in your brand voice with a tab per account. Follow up with "make it shorter", "more playful" or "schedule it".

## Past chats

The clock icon in the panel lists every chat. Open one to carry on, or delete it.
