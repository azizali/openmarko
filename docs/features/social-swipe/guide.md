# Swipe file: how to

Your swipe file keeps formats and ideas worth reusing, each broken into its beats.

- Save trends from the Social scanner with **Save to swipe file**.
- Choose **New idea** to add your own: name it, say why it works, list the beats, and pick the best channels.
- Starter ideas are included to get you going.

Choose **Create post** on any idea to write it in your voice for each account.
