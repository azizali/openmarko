# Ad research: how to

## See what works for others

1. Open **Ads → Ad research** and search by advertiser, keyword or service, plus a place.
2. **What's working** summarises ads that have run 30 days or more (the ones paying for themselves): common angles, offers, formats, and angles nobody is using.
3. Sort the table by days running; **Long runner** marks the proven ones.
4. Open an ad to see why it works and how your version could sound.

## Keep the good ones

Select ads and choose **Save to swipe file**, or **Make my versions** to go straight to Creative studio. On the **Ad swipe file** tab, add your own ads or any ad by link, with a note.
