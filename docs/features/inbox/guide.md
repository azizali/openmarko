# Inbox: how to

1. Open **Inbox**. Each person has one thread, whatever channel they used.
2. **Needs reply** shows people waiting on you. **Calls to make** lists calls your workflows asked the front desk to make, each with a script.
3. Reply by text or email, log a call, or add a note from the same box. When it's their turn, the assistant has a draft ready to edit.

To let the assistant answer simple questions (hours, insurance, directions, the booking link) on its own, change **Answer simple questions in the Inbox** in Settings → Autopilot.
