# Creative studio: how to

## Make a batch

1. Open **Ads → Creative studio**.
2. Fill the brief: ads to learn from, your offer and landing page, the angles to try, how many hooks per angle, formats and sizes, and looks.
3. Check the count and cost shown, then generate.

## Pick the winners

Each creative has a score and checks (ad platform rules, headline lengths, your brand rules). Approve or reject them one by one, select several, or choose **Approve 75+ that pass**. Open one to see every size and tweak the copy. **More like these** makes another batch on the same angles.

## Publish

Choose **Publish**, pick the platform, and either start a new test campaign or add to an existing one. Choose one ad set per angle or all together, the daily budget, locations, and whether to start paused. You'll be warned if it takes daily budgets over your cap.
