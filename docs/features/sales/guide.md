# Prospecting: how to

Available to agencies.

## Find businesses to pitch

1. Open **Prospecting**. Enter a keyword or category and a city, ZIP or coordinates. Use **Bulk** to paste several keywords and places at once.
2. Sort by **ease of sale** to find the best targets; the top one is tagged **Easiest sale**. Missing $/mo estimates the revenue they're losing.
3. Filter to **Not contacted yet** or **Verified email**.
4. Expand a row to see their map coverage against the top 3, the quickest fixes, and contact details.

Every search is saved on the left; reopening one is free. Pin the ones you use often.

To target businesses with no website, use **Only businesses with no website**. To audit one specific business, choose **Audit a business**.

## Audit a prospect

Choose **Audit** on a row. The audit shows their visibility score, how they compare with the top 3, a map of where they show up, profile and website gaps, whether AI assistants recommend them, a step-by-step plan, and a pitch email to copy. Print it to PDF if needed.

## Share an audit

Choose **Share link**. The shared page is written to the business owner, carries your brand, and leaves out contact details and the pitch. You'll see when they open it, on what device, for how long and which sections they read. Turn the link off any time.

## Send to outreach

Select prospects and choose **Send to campaign**. See the Outreach guide.
