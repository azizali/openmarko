# Citations & backlinks: how to

## Fix your listings

1. Open **Citations & backlinks**. The health score shows how consistent your business details are across the directories that matter.
2. Each directory shows whether you're listed correctly, listed with wrong details, missing, or duplicated, and whether competitors are there.
3. Directories marked as cited by AI assistants are worth fixing first.
4. Select listings and choose **Fix & build**. Each row says how it gets fixed and what it costs.

## Earn more links

On the **Backlinks** tab, see how many sites link to you compared with the leader, which links are new or lost, and sites that link to two or more competitors but not you. Each opportunity has a drafted outreach email.
