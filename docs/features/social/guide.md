# Social media: how to

The Social area has three parts:

- **Social scanner**: conversations to join, trends to remix, and standout posts from profiles you watch.
- **Swipe file**: formats and ideas worth reusing.
- **Posts & calendar**: everything you've drafted, scheduled and published.

Any idea can become a post: choose **Remix in my voice** or **Create post**, pick the accounts, adjust the tone, and check the preview of how it will look on each platform before you schedule it.

Connect your accounts in **Settings → Integrations → Social**. You can use the scanner and swipe file without connecting anything.
