# AI answers: how to

## See if AI assistants recommend you

Open **AI answers**. The score shows how often ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google's AI answers name you for the questions you track, overall and by engine.

- **Who AI recommends instead** shows the businesses named most often.
- **Sites AI answers cite** shows where those answers get their information, and whether you're listed there. Getting listed on those sites is often the quickest win (see Citations).
- Open a question to read each engine's answer.

## Track a new question

Choose **New question** and type it the way a customer would ask, for example "best emergency dentist near South Congress".
