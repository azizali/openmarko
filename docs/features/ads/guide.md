# Ads: how to

## Set your goal

1. Open **Ads → Campaigns** and connect an ad account if you haven't (Meta, Google or TikTok).
2. Under **Goal & guardrails**, pick what counts as a result (a booked appointment, a form lead, or a call over 60 seconds), the cost per result you're aiming for, and your monthly cap.
3. Set how often the assistant reviews, the biggest budget change it may make in one step, how much an ad must spend before it's judged, and whether it may launch new ads without asking.

For results to mean bookings rather than clicks, connect your ad tracking (Pixel, Google conversions, call tracking, booking system) in Settings → Integrations → Ad tracking.

## Act on recommendations

After each review, **Recommends** lists changes with the numbers behind them and what should happen. Apply one, dismiss it, or apply all budget and pause changes at once. In **Auto within guardrails** mode, safe changes are applied for you and logged.

## Check performance

**Performance, 7 days** shows every campaign, ad set or ad. Filter by platform or verdict (Losing, Wearing out, Winning…). Turn rows on or off, edit budgets inline, or select rows to pause, save to the swipe file, or make variations.

## Undo a change

**Changes** lists everything you or the assistant changed and what happened after. Choose **Undo** on any of them.

## Refresh tired ads

When an ad is wearing out, choose **Make fresh versions** to open Creative studio with it as the reference.
