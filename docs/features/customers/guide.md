# Contacts: how to

## Find people

Open **Contacts**. Lists on the left are saved filters (new leads, ad leads, due for a cleaning, no-shows…). Sort the table by any column, or switch to **Pipeline** to see people by stage and drag them between stages.

## Do something with a group

Select people, then text or email them (this starts a campaign), start a workflow, move their stage, tag them or export them.

## See everything about one person

Open a contact to see their details, what they've agreed to receive (email, texts), tags and every message, call and visit on one timeline. Reply from the box at the bottom, or open the conversation in the Inbox.
