# Texting: how to

1. Open **Settings → Email & texting → Texting & calls setup**.
2. Choose built-in numbers, or connect your Twilio or Telnyx account.
3. For each location, get a new local number, text-enable the number you already have, or port it over.
4. Confirm the carrier registration (it's prefilled from your business details). Texts can't go out until it's approved.
5. Set quiet hours (no texts overnight) and your opt-out line.
6. Choose where calls ring, whether to record them, and whether voicemail is turned into text.
