# Social scanner: how to

## Set what to listen for

Choose **Settings** (or the status line under the title).

- **Reply** side: pick sources (Reddit, Quora, Facebook groups, X, Nextdoor, and more), the places to watch on each (subreddits, groups, neighbourhoods), and the terms to listen for: your services, your name, competitors.
- **Trending** side: pick trend sources, topics, your area, anything never to suggest, and how good a fit a trend must be.

## Join conversations

On **Reply**, you'll see people asking for what you offer and posts that mention you, best fit first. Open one to read the post, why it's worth replying, and a reply drafted in your voice that follows that community's rules. Edit it, then **Copy reply & open** to post it yourself on the platform. Mark it **Replied** or **Not for us**.

## Use a trend

On **Trending**, open a trend to see why it works and how your version could go. **Save to swipe file** to keep it, or **Remix in my voice** to write a post now.

## Learn from other accounts

On **Watching**, see posts from competitors and accounts you admire that did much better than usual. Add or remove profiles at the bottom.
