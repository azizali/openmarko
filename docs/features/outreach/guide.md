# Outreach: how to

Available to agencies.

## Connect your sequencer

1. **Settings → Outreach** (or **Outreach → Connect**). Choose PlusVibe, Instantly or Smartlead and paste your API key.
2. Check your sending inboxes and their warmup.
3. Pick a default campaign, or create the ready-made 3-step local audit campaign that includes each prospect's audit link.

## Send prospects

1. In **Prospecting**, select prospects (or open one) and choose **Send to campaign**.
2. Prospects with no email, an unverified email, or already in a campaign are skipped.
3. Preview each email filled in with that prospect's numbers, then send. Each gets a share link to their audit.

Turn off the review step to send single prospects in one click.

## Handle replies

**Outreach → Replies** shows every reply from every inbox. Their audit opens beside the thread. Label replies (Interested, Meeting booked, Not interested) and send the assistant's drafted reply from the same inbox the thread started in.
